# Role positioning

These are emphasis choices for the same evidence, not different research claims. Use [resume bullets](RESUME_BULLETS.md) with the [claims registry](CLAIMS_EVIDENCE.md).

| Audience | Lead with | Supporting evidence | De-emphasize | Main caveat to retain |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DV / microstructure QR | Order reconstruction, imbalance/OFI/microprice, tree models | 85.8M messages, 56.9M rows, paired uplift, spread/latency tests | Broad ML-library lists | Predictive IC is not executable profit |
| AQR-style QR | Fixed chronology, negative controls, equal-weight blocks | 20/20, 18/20, 18/20 block wins; positive deletion checks | Hardware details and speed as the headline | Descriptive robustness, not formal significance |
| PIMCO-style QR | Historical time-series pipeline and reproducibility | Partitioned cache, complete task manifests, robust baseline comparison | Dense high-frequency vocabulary | Five historical equities; no universal evidence |
| General QR | End-to-end research plus engineering | Three horizons, 152 tasks, transfer and execution diagnostics | Implementation trivia | Modest incremental model result |
| Quant Trading | Spread, timing and why a forecast may not be tradable | Negative crossed-book diagnostics, passive-fill uncertainty | A long metric table | No live trading or realized PnL |

Recommended default: the General QR compact bullet when role detail is limited. Use the DV detailed pair when order-book research and engineering are central. For statistics-heavy roles, lead with AQR-style design discipline; for broad time-series research, use the PIMCO-style version. Keep only numbers that can be explained immediately from an evidence file.

The completed queue study can support a technical discussion of missing execution labels and conditional fills. It should not replace the clear baseline story or become a claim of a production matching engine.
