# PM1B 15-second snapshot repricing, pre-fit model protocol

Status: **FROZEN BEFORE PM1B MODEL FIT; FINAL TARGET CLOSED**. PM0 source/version, market-grouped chronological split, PM1A decision opportunities, and the PM1B 15-second future-book horizon are pinned in the prior public manifests. PM1A v1 terminal final has already been scored under its own protocol; it is separate from this unopened PM1B repricing final.

The strict future Up book is captured after each decision, no later than decision+15 seconds, at most five seconds stale relative to the target clock, and valid two-sided/unlocked/uncrossed. Target is future midpoint minus current midpoint in probability points, not terminal outcome or an exchange-event return. Train has 392 markets/12,479 opportunities; dev 234/7,476. The outcome-blind audit predicts final 120/4,104, but its repricing values remain unopened. These counts are not independent time regimes: the source spans only four sampled UTC dates, with a partial final date.

`configs/polymarket_pm1b_model_v1.json` freezes one model configuration per arm and no search grid. B0 predicts zero repricing. B1 is current-state XGBoost on the ten current features. B2 is history XGBoost on the flattened eight-by-eleven causal tensor. B3 is a one-layer hidden-32 GRU on that exact same history with seeds 7/17/29, at most 60 epochs, patience 8, AdamW 0.001/0.0001 and batch 256. Train-only feature/target standardization and equal total weight per market apply. Dev equal-market MSE alone chooses neural checkpoints. No Transformer, extra horizon or future-target feature is added.

Primary score is equal-market mean MSE, with B3 three-seed prediction mean minus B2 as the primary paired market contrast. B2−B1 and each versus B0 are secondary. Resample whole markets in 5,000 paired bootstrap draws with fixed seeds; these intervals condition on the few observed UTC dates. Report date and contract-length descriptive strata, coverage, all seeds and all null/negative/failed results. Before final reveal, a 1% relative dev-market-MSE improvement over B2 is the practical preference threshold for B3. Final scoring still reports all frozen arms regardless of the dev preference; a positive final claim also needs the paired market interval's upper bound below zero, and remains a narrow within-source pilot.

The final target may be built and opened **once** only after code/config, the complete dev report, and fitted-model hashes are committed and pushed. Final uses those exact train/dev-fitted models, with no refit, retuning, threshold movement or resealing. Source rows, target arrays, per-market scores, predictions, weights, and live account data remain private. Snapshot repricing is not terminal information, actual fills, PnL, or WSE independent confirmation; no résumé/PDF edit follows automatically.
