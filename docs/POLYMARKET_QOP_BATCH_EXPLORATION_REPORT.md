## Measurement and missingness

At receive anchor `r`, verified passive direction `d` and receipt leg price `q`, G = 100d(mid immediately before r − q), D_h = 100d(mid at r+h − mid before r), N_h = G+D_h, in cents/share. The latest *raw* quote is chosen first, then numeric validity and age are checked; no older fallback, no zero fill. Tied receive timestamps with conflicting BBO, clock reversals, planned event end and stale quotes are NA. The 5s primary eligible share fraction is 21,385.630856 / 26,095.716846 = 81.95% of joined target-leg shares. The 30s fraction is 14,370.435334 / 26,095.716846 = 55.07%. Fail reason counts overlap and are recorded in `build_audit.json`. If missing 5s midpoint only is bounded to [0,1], all 1,754 known legs have a share-weighted N bound of **[−7.888,+10.161] cents/share**; this wide interval leaves even its sign unresolved. The 250ms sample is exactly the common age sample because stricter eligibility nests in primary; its +1.049 headline is not a same-row improvement.

Feature cutoffs are actual public receive times r−1s and r−5s, with a fixed 30s history. For each cutoff, BUY and SELL candidate vectors are generated from then-known public state; the later receipt direction merely selects a candidate for evaluation. Maker size, price, role and future quote never enter features. A synthetic future quote/print mutation left past features unchanged. Individual cutoff feature vectors exist for 1,210/1,158 legs, but only 781 have valid 5s labels **and both** complete cutoff feature sets. Stale books (723 issue incidences), stale quotes (637), conflicts (155) and other failures are explicit; counts can overlap. This coverage is a conditioning choice, not evidence that unavailable books would have equal outcomes.

The shallow arm's late N reduction alongside worse calibration and weak 5s result is a fragile exploratory contrast. The late block is still the same exposed date, so these are no independent final or significance claims.

For event e, J_e = sum(observed model-ready retained leg shares × observed N)/**all receipt-joined target-leg sampled shares V_e**. J_all uses all model-ready legs with observed N; missing legs contribute neither a value nor an invented zero outcome. At 100% retention J equals J_all by definition, with mean retained raw-share fraction just 35.1%. The 75% calibration score thresholds retain materially less than 75% of raw sampled shares. Example one-second M0 abandons +0.108 cents/raw share of observed positive N at 75%; M1 +0.272, M2 +0.270, shallow +0.198. J−J_all is positive for the four one-second arms (+0.559/+0.733/+0.510/+0.751), but J itself remains negative and is contingent on already observed fills. At 50%, shallow J is +0.085 on only 12.1% of raw shares; this secondary, late exploratory slice cannot justify choosing it after inspection. The JSON preserves every 50/75/90/100 result, including adverse/null values and actual retention.

Gross midpoint N is not spread capture alone, realized maker return, a counterfactual cancel/quote policy, or a size-verified bid/ask exit. G may contain undisclosed pre-receipt motion; D is only post-receipt motion. Quote-side exit sensitivity is arithmetic against posted prices and has no verified displayed size. The raw fee field is kept unconverted because denomination and rebate accounting have not been independently audited. No causal execution cost or net compensation is asserted.
